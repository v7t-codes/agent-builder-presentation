# Market framing: the problem and segmentation

## The problem these tools are solving

- Baseline capability — model + loop + tool calling — is not production readiness.
- Production automation requires connectors into real systems, permissioning, auditability, eval gates, and reliable execution environments.
- The most durable value shows up where the platform reduces operator load, not where it adds more prompt routing.

## How we break the market down

We break the market into five segments:

1) **Developer & SDK-first:** runtimes, SDKs/frameworks, and computer-use tooling.  
2) **No-code & visual:** UI builders and automation incumbents evolving into agent builders.  
3) **Vertical-specific platforms:** domain outcomes with deep integrations and KPI-linked eval loops.  
4) **Agent studios & “Human+ software”:** outcome delivery plus operations, with reusable internal tooling.  
5) **AI employees / digital workers:** cross-segment packaging of agents as labor.

## How we compare products

These four axes make the autonomy regime explicit:

- We classify the environment surface as API, browser, desktop, multi-surface, or voice.
- We classify task complexity as low, medium, or high.
- We classify time sensitivity as batch, operational, or real-time.
- We classify risk and consequence as low, medium, or high.
